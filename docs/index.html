Checkout redesign experiment

Variant B simplifies checkout. 80,000 users, randomized 50/50, six weeks of synthetic data. Analysed with product-analytics-toolkit.

Ship it: conversion is up +8.1% (95% CI +0.12% to +0.75% absolute), with no sample ratio mismatch.
